# Case Study

PromiseGap demonstrates enterprise SaaS product thinking, Technical PM execution, AI product judgment, human-in-the-loop design, and customer trust risk management.

Problem: teams promise capabilities across calls, proposals, demos, and onboarding notes without a single review workflow against product reality.

MVP decision: prioritize extraction, capability evidence, transparent matching, risk scoring, owner assignment, and reports.

Trade-off: use explainable keyword matching for the MVP rather than opaque semantic matching.

Demo scenario: Acme Pharma hears that protected Microsoft Graph profile photos will sync automatically from Entra ID. PromiseGap detects the promise, matches it to a planned capability, flags partial support, scores high risk, and recommends product review plus customer clarification.

Future roadmap: embeddings, richer ingestion, CRM integrations, Slack/Teams workflows, roadmap conversion, analytics, and governance policies.

Resume positioning: Designed and built PromiseGap, a human-in-the-loop B2B SaaS product that extracts customer promises from sales materials, maps them to verified product capabilities, and flags unsupported commitments before onboarding or contract finalization.
