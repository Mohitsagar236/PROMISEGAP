# PRD

## Objective

Help B2B SaaS teams resolve high-risk promise gaps before onboarding or contract finalization.

## Target Users

Product Managers, Technical PMs, Sales, Customer Success, Implementation, Solution Architects, and founders.

## Problem

Customer commitments are scattered across sales calls, SOWs, proposals, emails, and onboarding notes. Teams often discover unsupported commitments too late.

## Jobs-to-Be-Done

When reviewing customer-facing materials, I want to identify unsupported promises so I can clarify scope, assign owners, and protect customer trust.

## MVP Scope

Authentication, demo workspace, accounts, documents, rule-based extraction, optional AI extraction, capability matching, risk scoring, review workflow, action items, reports, and settings.

## Non-Goals

Generic CRM, chatbot-only experience, full contract lifecycle management, billing system, or fully automated AI decisioning.

## Acceptance Criteria

Users can login, load demo data, ingest documents, extract promises, match capabilities, score risk, assign owners, move workflow statuses, create action items, generate reports, and view a product case study.

## Metrics

North star: high-risk promise gaps resolved before onboarding or contract finalization.

Supporting metrics: unsupported promises detected, validation completion, average time to resolution, repeated unsupported promises by capability area, and match acceptance rate.

## Risks

False positives, stale capability evidence, unclear ownership, overreliance on AI, and incomplete source coverage.

## Open Questions

Which CRM and call-recording integrations produce the highest-value ingestion path? Which roles should approve contractual wording?
